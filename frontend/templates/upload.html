Detection Workflow

Step 1 : Video Upload

User uploads surveillance video.

Step 2 : Frame Extraction

OpenCV extracts frames from the uploaded video.

Step 3 : Person Detection

YOLOv8 detects people and suspicious objects.

Step 4 : Feature Extraction

ResNet50 extracts deep visual features.

Step 5 : Temporal Analysis

LSTM learns motion patterns across frames.

Step 6 : Anomaly Detection

Spatio-Temporal Autoencoder classifies abnormal activities.

Step 7 : Alert Generation

Alarm, snapshot capture and database logging.

About AnomaliNet

AnomaliNet is an AI-powered intelligent surveillance system developed to detect abnormal human activities in CCTV videos.

The system combines YOLOv8, ResNet50, LSTM and a Spatio-Temporal Autoencoder to recognize anomalies in real time.

Whenever suspicious activity is detected, the system automatically captures snapshots, stores evidence in the database and triggers alerts.
